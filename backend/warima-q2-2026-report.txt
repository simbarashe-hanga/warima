# Warima Q2 2026 Report

**April – June 2026**

## Executive Summary

Q2 2026 was a foundational quarter focused on transforming Warima from a prototype into a pilot-ready agricultural commerce platform.

During the quarter, the team shifted from an Android-first strategy to a WhatsApp-first experience, significantly reducing onboarding friction for farmers while aligning the product with existing user behaviour. Alongside this transition, conversational AI capabilities were integrated, PaymentOS infrastructure progressed toward operational readiness, and supply-side operations continued while livestock production matured.

Although no revenue was generated during the quarter, the primary objective was reducing execution risk ahead of pilot deployment rather than short-term sales.

# Key Learnings

Several important lessons emerged during Q2.

### 1. Product alone is not enough

Building software is only one part of the value chain. Successful agricultural pilots require operational systems—including logistics, supplier coordination, and delivery—to mature alongside the technology.

### 2. Customer demand matters more than early revenue

Revenue has not yet begun; however, customer interest through WhatsApp and the pork waiting list demonstrates meaningful market demand. Demand validation reduces commercial risk before scaling production.

### 3. Meet farmers where they already are

The decision to move from Android to WhatsApp substantially lowers adoption barriers and better reflects existing communication habits among target users.
